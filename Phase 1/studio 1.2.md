Title = Beyond the Horizon
Create a digital illustration for a Synthwave/Retro 1980s promotional album cover featuring a futuristic sports car driving toward a glowing neon city at sunset. Use deep shadows in the foreground with brilliant pink, purple, blue, and orange neon light focused around the car and horizon to create strong value and contrast. Create an illusion of space using overlapping elements, a large car in the foreground, and smaller, faded mountains and buildings in the distance. Show time and motion through glowing speed lines, stretched reflections, and light trails behind the moving car. Use a consistent neon color palette and geometric shapes to create unity, while adding one unexpected floating geometric shape for variety. Use asymmetrical balance, with the oversized car as the main focal point and the city and sunset balancing the composition. Create a clear hierarchy of scale by making foreground elements larger and background elements smaller and less detailed. Keep the design cinematic, polished, atmospheric, and retro-futuristic, with no text or logos.

Definitions:
Value :The use of light and dark area in an artwork to create contrast, depth and visual interest.
Space:The area between objects that create a sense of depth and distances in an art.
Unity: Imposition of order/ harmony created on design using principles that brings all elements of an artwork together. 
Balance: refers to distribution of elements within work to create sense of stability.
Rhythm: Repitition of visual elements such as lines, shapes, colors or pattern, that creates a sense of movement .
